---
title: 'Triggers for custom datasources (Experimental)'
categories: ['API']
---

Datasources you index through the Indexing API can now have triggers via the Triggers API, with presets generated from their datasource schema. Custom datasource trigger presets are experimental and are turned on per datasource. To turn them on, contact your Glean account team.

{/* truncate */}

Triggers endpoints still require `X-Glean-Include-Experimental: true`. See [Custom datasources](/guides/triggers/overview#custom-datasources) for the presets and inputs each object type gets.
