<RecipeSection label="Problem">

Building RAG over your own content usually means standing up a vector DB,
mirroring every source system's ACLs into it, and keeping that mirror in
sync forever — get any of that wrong and you leak documents. Glean is
already the permission-aware index of your company's content. Use its
Platform API as retrieval and skip all three problems.

</RecipeSection>
